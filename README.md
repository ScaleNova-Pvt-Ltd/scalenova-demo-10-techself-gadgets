Inspired by modern gadget eCommerce architectures (e.g. `techself.demos.tailgrids.com`), **TechSelf Gadgets** is a high-performance electronics store showcasing spatial audio, wearables, and camera hardware.

---

## 1. Key Highlights
- **Flash Sale Countdown**: Real-time ticker and deal-of-the-day callout banner.
- **Cart & Fast Checkout Flow**: Slide-out cart drawer with instant subtotal and multi-field express order placement.
- **ScaleNova Master Lead Ingestion**: Synchronized via Google Apps Script to `Demo 10 - TechSelf`.

## 2. Integrated Pages
1. `index.html`: Hero with 3D product view, countdown banner, trending products, and quick-add.
2. `products.html`: Complete gadget store catalog.
3. `checkout.html`: Express order checkout with shipping destination and contact verification.
4. `about.html`: Laboratory testing, acoustic engineering, and 2-year exchange warranty.
5. `contact.html`: 24/7 technical support desk.
6. `terms.html` & `privacy.html`: Hardware consumer terms and warranty regulations.
